## The basics

<Accordions type="single">

<Accordion title="What exactly is a PT and a YT?">
When you deposit, Spield mints equal amounts of a **Principal Token (PT)** and a **Yield Token
(YT)**. PT is a zero-coupon bond that redeems 1:1 for your principal at maturity. YT is a claim
on all the yield that position generates until maturity. Together they always equal the value
of the original position. → [PT and YT explained](/docs/how-it-works/pt-and-yt)
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Where does the yield actually come from?">
From **Blend**, a native Stellar lending market. Your USDC becomes a Blend supply position
whose exchange rate genuinely rises on-chain as borrowers pay interest. There's no invented
index and no bridged asset — the backing grows with the real rate. →
[The yield source](/docs/how-it-works/yield-source)
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Do I need to understand PT and YT to use Spield?">
No. The **Fixed-Rate Vault** lets you deposit USDC and lock a guaranteed payout without ever
touching PT or YT. PT/YT are there for users who want more control. →
[Fixed-Rate Vault](/docs/features/fixed-rate-vault)
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="What do I deposit?">
**USDC** — Circle's USD-backed stablecoin, native on Stellar. You'll also want a small amount
of **XLM** for tiny network fees. → [Funding your wallet](/docs/getting-started/funding-your-wallet)
</Accordion>

</Accordions>

## Returns and risk

<Accordions type="single">

<Accordion title="Can I lose money holding PT?">
PT redeems 1:1 for principal at maturity, so held to maturity it returns your principal plus
the locked discount as fixed yield. Before maturity its market price moves with rates, like
any bond — so selling early could be at a gain or a loss. YT carries more risk: it can decay
to zero if realized yield underperforms the implied APY.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Is the fixed rate really guaranteed?">
For a payout already issued (a vault receipt, or PT you've bought), yes — it doesn't change.
The vault only ever promises a payout it already holds the backing for, and PT redeems 1:1 at
maturity. The *quoted* rate for **new** deposits can change over time, but never retroactively.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="What are the main risks?">
The honest short list: **smart-contract risk** (as with any DeFi), **yield-source risk** (Spield
depends on the underlying lending market staying healthy), **YT risk** (it can decay to zero),
and **market risk** (PT's price moves before maturity). Full detail:
[Understanding the risks](/docs/resources/security).
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Could the protocol become insolvent?">
By design, no: backing only grows, yield payouts come from that growth (never principal), and
the solvency invariant is checked on every operation. You can verify it live on the
[Solvency dashboard](/docs/features/solvency-dashboard).
</Accordion>

</Accordions>

## Mechanics

<Accordions type="single">

<Accordion title="Can I exit before maturity?">
Yes. Sell PT (or YT) on the market at the current price, or — if you hold equal PT + YT —
recombine them back into USDC any time. The fixed return is what you get if you *hold to
maturity*; exiting early means taking the current market price.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="How do I claim my YT yield?">
Open your position and click **Claim**. Spield pays the accrued yield in USDC and your YT keeps
earning to maturity. You can claim as often as you like.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Do LPs suffer impermanent loss?">
The fixed-maturity, time-decay design largely eliminates it: as maturity approaches, PT
converges to par and YT decays to zero, so an LP who stays to maturity faces near-zero
impermanent loss while still earning swap fees. → [Liquidity](/docs/features/liquidity)
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="What fees does Spield charge?">
The market charges a small **swap fee** on trades (bounded by an on-chain ceiling), which goes
to liquidity providers. There are also the usual tiny **Stellar network fees** in XLM. Holding
PT to maturity or using the vault involves no trading fee.
</Accordion>

</Accordions>

## Trust and safety

<Accordions type="single">

<Accordion title="Is there any bridge or cross-chain risk in the core?">
No. Spield's core protocol is **Stellar-native only** — both the deposit and settlement asset
(USDC) live on Stellar, with no EVM bridge or relayer in the value path. The optional
[bridge feature](/docs/features/cross-chain-bridge) only helps you *get* USDC onto Stellar; it's
not part of how your position is held.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Who controls the protocol? Can an admin take my funds?">
No admin can move user funds, mint unbacked tokens, or drain reserves — those paths don't
exist. Admin powers are limited to bounded actions (pausing *new* inflows, adjusting
rates/fees within on-chain ceilings, and timelocked upgrades). A pause can never trap your
funds — exits always stay open. → [Architecture: trust model](/docs/developers/architecture#trust-model-honest-summary)
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Has Spield been audited?">
Treat Spield as an early-stage protocol and check the current status in the
[Resources](/docs/resources) and the app before committing significant funds. As always in
DeFi, only deposit what you're comfortable putting at risk.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="How can I verify the protocol is solvent right now?">
Open the [Solvency dashboard](/docs/features/solvency-dashboard) in the app, or query the
solvency figures directly from the chain yourself — see
[reading state](/docs/developers/reading-state). The numbers are live and permissionless to
read.
</Accordion>
