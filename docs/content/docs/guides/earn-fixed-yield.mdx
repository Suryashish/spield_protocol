Locking a fixed return is Spield's core promise. There are two ways to do it: the **easy
way** (the Fixed-Rate Vault) and the **hands-on way** (buy PT directly). Both lock a known
return; pick based on how much control you want.

<Tabs items={['Easy: Fixed-Rate Vault', 'Hands-on: buy PT']}>

<Tab value="Easy: Fixed-Rate Vault">

The vault hides all the machinery. Deposit USDC, get a receipt for a guaranteed payout.

<Steps>

<Step>
### Open the Fixed Vault

In the app sidebar, select **Fixed Vault**.
</Step>

<Step>
### Enter your amount

Type the USDC you want to deposit. You'll see your **guaranteed payout at maturity** and the
**fixed APY** you're locking.
</Step>

<Step>
### Deposit

Click **Deposit** and approve in your wallet. Your first deposit may include a one-time
trustline approval — that's normal.
</Step>

<Step>
### Redeem at maturity

After the maturity date, return to the vault and **redeem** your receipt for the full payout
in USDC.
</Step>

</Steps>

<Callout title="Done — your return is locked">
  From the moment you deposit, your payout is fixed. Nothing the market does afterward changes
  it.
</Callout>

</Tab>

<Tab value="Hands-on: buy PT">

Buying PT yourself lets you hold the bond directly (and trade it later if you want).

<Steps>

<Step>
### Open the Markets page

Select **Markets** and make sure you're on the **Earn Fixed** tab.
</Step>

<Step>
### Check the implied APY

The headline **implied APY** is the fixed return you'd lock by buying PT now and holding to
maturity. If you like it, continue.
</Step>

<Step>
### Enter an amount and review the quote

Enter how much USDC to spend. The app shows how much PT you'll receive and your effective
fixed rate. Set a **slippage tolerance** if you wish.
</Step>

<Step>
### Buy and hold

Confirm the swap and approve in your wallet. You now hold PT. Hold it to maturity, then
**redeem** it 1:1 for USDC on the Markets/Positions area.
</Step>

</Steps>

<Callout type="info" title="Exiting early">
  Want out before maturity? Sell your PT back on the market at the current price, or if you
  also hold matching YT, recombine them to USDC. Either way you take the *current* price, not
  the locked one.
</Callout>

</Tab>

</Tabs>

## How your return is calculated

Your fixed return comes from the **discount**: you acquire principal-worth-1.00 for less than
1.00 today, and it's worth exactly 1.00 at maturity.

> Example: buy PT at **0.97** with **90 days** to maturity. At maturity it's worth **1.00**.
> That's a **~3.1% return over 90 days** (≈ 12.5% annualized) — locked in now.

## What can go wrong?

- **You sell early at a loss.** Before maturity, PT's price moves with rates. If you sell early when rates have risen, you might get less than you paid. Held to maturity, you get par.
- **Yield-source risk.** Spield's backing depends on the underlying lending market; see [risks](/docs/resources/security).

That's it — the simplest, lowest-risk way to use Spield. Next, the opposite bet:
[go long on yield](/docs/guides/long-yield).
