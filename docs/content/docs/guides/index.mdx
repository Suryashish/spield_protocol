## Choosing a guide

| Your goal | Guide | Risk |
| --- | --- | :---: |
| A predictable, guaranteed return | [Earn a fixed yield](/docs/guides/earn-fixed-yield) | Low |
| Bet that yields will rise | [Go long on yield](/docs/guides/long-yield) | Higher |
| Earn income from trading activity | [Provide liquidity](/docs/guides/provide-liquidity) | Medium |
| Just get USDC onto Stellar | [Bridge USDC](/docs/guides/bridge-usdc) | — |
