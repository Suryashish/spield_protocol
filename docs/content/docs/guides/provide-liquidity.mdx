Providing liquidity earns you a share of every swap fee. Thanks to Spield's time-decay market,
LPs who hold to maturity face **near-zero impermanent loss** — see
[Liquidity](/docs/features/liquidity) for why.

## Before you start

You'll need both **PT and USDC**. If you don't hold PT yet, get some by
[minting PT + YT](/docs/features/pt-yt-tokens) or buying PT on the market first.

## Add liquidity

<Steps>

<Step>
### Open the Liquidity page

Select **Liquidity** in the app sidebar.
</Step>

<Step>
### Enter an amount

Enter how much you want to provide. The app **auto-mirrors** the matching amount of the other
asset at the pool's current ratio — so you add balanced liquidity without moving the price.
</Step>

<Step>
### Confirm

Approve in your wallet. You receive **LP shares** representing your slice of the pool.
</Step>

<Step>
### Earn fees

From the next trade onward, your shares accrue a portion of swap fees. Your claim on the pool
grows over time.
</Step>

</Steps>

## Monitor your position

The Liquidity page shows your **shares**, the **PT + USDC** they currently redeem for, and the
fees earned. Because the pool's composition shifts as people trade, your underlying PT/USDC
split will move around — that's normal.

## Remove liquidity

<Steps>

<Step>
### Choose how much to withdraw

Use the percentage slider to remove part or all of your position.
</Step>

<Step>
### Confirm

Approve in your wallet. You receive your proportional **PT and USDC** back, including accrued
fees.
</Step>

</Steps>

<Callout title="You can always exit">
  Removing liquidity is never blocked — not at maturity, not during an emergency pause. There's
  no lock-up.
</Callout>

## Tips

- **Hold to maturity for the best result.** That's when the near-zero-IL property is strongest, because PT's drift to par is fully priced in.
- **Returns track volume.** More trading through the pool means more fees for LPs.
- **Add at the current ratio.** The app does this for you; it keeps things fair and avoids price impact.

<Callout type="warn" title="Impermanent loss isn't fully zero">
  "Near-zero" applies to PT's predictable march to par when you hold to maturity. Unexpected
  yield swings can still cause some divergence if you exit early. See
  [risks](/docs/resources/security).
</Callout>
