Every term you'll meet in these docs, defined simply. Linked to the page where each is used in
context.

### AMM (Automated Market Maker)
A smart contract that lets people trade against a pool of assets at an algorithmic price,
instead of matching buyers and sellers directly. Spield's [market](/docs/features/yield-market)
is a time-decay AMM.

### Backing
The live value of the protocol's yield position — the real assets standing behind PT and YT.
Spield keeps backing ≥ everything it owes. See [Solvency](/docs/how-it-works/solvency).

### Base units
The integer representation of a token amount on-chain. Spield's tokens use 7 decimals, so
`10_000_000` base units = `1.0`. See [Tokens & units](/docs/developers/tokens).

### Blend
Stellar's primary lending market, where Spield sources its yield by supplying USDC. See
[The yield source](/docs/how-it-works/yield-source).

### Bridge
Infrastructure for moving assets between blockchains. Spield offers an optional
[cross-chain bridge](/docs/features/cross-chain-bridge) to bring USDC to Stellar; the core
protocol itself uses no bridge.

### Coupon
The fixed extra amount a Fixed-Rate Vault deposit earns on top of principal — the "interest"
portion of your guaranteed payout.

### Discount
How far below par (1.0) a PT trades today. The discount is the source of PT's fixed return.

### Fixed APY
The annualized return you lock in by buying PT (or depositing in the vault) and holding to
maturity. It doesn't change after you commit.

### Fixed-Rate Vault
Spield's flagship product: deposit USDC, receive a receipt for a guaranteed payout at maturity.
See [Fixed-Rate Vault](/docs/features/fixed-rate-vault).

### Implied APY
The market's *current* expectation of future yield, read off the PT price. Buying YT is a bet
that **realized** yield will beat the **implied** APY.

### Impermanent loss (IL)
The opportunity cost an LP can face when pool asset prices move. Spield's time-decay design
makes IL **near zero** for LPs who hold to maturity. See [Liquidity](/docs/features/liquidity).

### LP / LP shares
A liquidity provider supplies assets to the market; **LP shares** represent their proportional
claim on the pool and its accrued fees.

### Maturity
The fixed date when PT becomes redeemable 1:1 for USDC and YT stops earning. Every Spield
market has one.

### Par
The face value of a PT — exactly 1 USDC. PT prices drift up toward par as maturity nears.

### Position
A single deposit, tracked with its own starting point for measuring yield. Precise
per-position accounting is why transferring/trading PT and YT is safe.

### PT (Principal Token)
The fixed-rate bond leg. Redeems 1:1 for USDC at maturity; bought at a discount today. Symbol
`SPLDPT`. See [PT and YT explained](/docs/how-it-works/pt-and-yt).

### Realized APY / yield
The yield that actually accrued (as opposed to the *implied* expectation). Reconstructable from
the yield-source rate over time.

### Redeem
To exchange a token for what it's owed: PT → 1 USDC at maturity, or a vault receipt → its full
payout.

### SAC (Stellar Asset Contract)
The smart-contract wrapper that lets a Stellar asset be used by Soroban contracts. PT, YT, and
USDC are all SACs. See [Tokens & units](/docs/developers/tokens).

### Slippage
The difference between a quoted price and the executed price on a trade. You set a tolerance so
trades only execute within an acceptable range.

### Solvency
The property that backing ≥ everything owed. Spield is solvent by construction and lets you
[verify it live](/docs/features/solvency-dashboard).

### Soroban
Stellar's smart-contract platform, on which Spield's contracts run.

### Stellar
The fast, low-fee blockchain Spield is built on. See [Why Stellar?](/docs/introduction/why-stellar).

### Stripping (yield stripping)
Splitting a yield-bearing position into a fixed leg (PT) and a variable leg (YT). The core
model behind Spield.

### Trustline
A Stellar account's declaration that it's willing to hold a particular asset. The app sets up
PT/YT trustlines for you automatically.

### USDC
Circle's USD-backed stablecoin, native on Stellar. The asset you deposit and PT redeems into.

### XLM
Stellar's native asset, used to pay small network fees and reserves.

### YT (Yield Token)
The variable yield leg — a claim on all yield to maturity. Cheap and leveraged; can decay to
zero. Symbol `SPLDYT`. See [PT and YT explained](/docs/how-it-works/pt-and-yt).
