Getting started with Spield takes three steps. This page is the map; each step has its
own short guide.

<DevPhaseNote />

<div className="fd-steps">

### Connect a wallet

Spield is used through a **Stellar wallet** browser extension. Connect it once and the app
remembers you. → [Connect a wallet](/docs/getting-started/connect-a-wallet)

### Fund your wallet with USDC

You deposit **USDC** into Spield. Get it onto Stellar from an exchange, or bring it from
another chain with the built-in bridge. → [Funding your wallet](/docs/getting-started/funding-your-wallet)

### Make your first deposit

Pick what you want — a **fixed return**, **leveraged yield**, or to **provide liquidity** —
and deposit. The first time, you'll click **Enable trustline** once (see
[Trustlines](/docs/getting-started/trustlines)), then deposit.
→ [Your first deposit](/docs/getting-started/your-first-deposit)

</div>

## What you'll need

| Requirement | Details |
| --- | --- |
| A **Stellar wallet** | A browser extension like Freighter (see the [full list](/docs/getting-started/connect-a-wallet)). |
| A little **XLM** | Stellar's native asset, used for tiny network fees. A fraction of an XLM is plenty. |
| Some **USDC** | The asset you deposit. Start with any amount you're comfortable with. |

<Callout title="New to Stellar wallets?">
  A wallet is just a browser extension that holds your keys and signs transactions. It's
  free, takes a minute to set up, and Spield never sees your secret key — you approve every
  action yourself.
</Callout>
