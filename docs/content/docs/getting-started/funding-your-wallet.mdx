To deposit into Spield you need two things in your wallet:

- **USDC** — the asset you deposit. This is Circle's official USD-backed stablecoin, native on Stellar.
- **A little XLM** — Stellar's native asset, used to pay tiny network fees and a small one-time reserve. A fraction of an XLM is enough.

## Getting USDC onto Stellar

There are two easy paths. Pick whichever fits where your funds are now.

<Tabs items={['From an exchange', 'Bridge from another chain']}>

<Tab value="From an exchange">

Many exchanges support **withdrawing USDC directly on the Stellar network**.

1. On the exchange, choose to withdraw **USDC**.
2. Select **Stellar** as the network (sometimes labelled "XLM network" or "Stellar Lumens").
3. Paste your Stellar wallet address as the destination.
4. Confirm and wait — Stellar settles in seconds.

You can buy a small amount of **XLM** the same way for fees.

<Callout type="warn" title="Pick the Stellar network">
  When withdrawing, you must choose the **Stellar** network specifically. Sending USDC on
  the wrong network (e.g. Ethereum) won't arrive in your Stellar wallet.
</Callout>

</Tab>

<Tab value="Bridge from another chain">

If your USDC is on **Ethereum, BSC, Polygon, Arbitrum, Solana, or Tron**, you can bring it
over without an exchange using Spield's built-in **cross-chain bridge** (powered by
Allbridge Core).

1. Open the **Bridge** page in the Spield app.
2. Choose the **source chain** and connect that chain's wallet.
3. Enter the amount of USDC, review the quote, and confirm.
4. Your USDC arrives as **native Stellar USDC**, ready to deposit.

See the full walkthrough: [Bridge USDC to Stellar](/docs/guides/bridge-usdc) and the
[Cross-chain bridge feature](/docs/features/cross-chain-bridge).

<Callout type="info">
  The bridge only moves funds **to** Stellar. Once your USDC is here, everything Spield does
  is fully native — there's no ongoing bridge dependency on your position.
</Callout>

</Tab>

</Tabs>

## A note on trustlines (it's automatic)

On Stellar, before an account can hold a particular asset it establishes a one-time
**trustline** to it. For USDC this usually happens when you first receive it. For Spield's
own tokens (PT and YT), **the app sets up the needed trustlines for you** with a single
**"Enable trustline"** click the first time you deposit — you don't have to do anything manual.

<Callout title="Want the details?">
  See the dedicated [Trustlines](/docs/getting-started/trustlines) page for exactly what a
  trustline is, why PT/YT need one, and how the one-click setup works.
</Callout>

## How much should I start with?

There's no minimum beyond covering fees. A sensible first run:

| Asset | Suggested starter amount |
| --- | --- |
| XLM | ~2–5 XLM (covers fees + reserves comfortably) |
| USDC | Whatever you're comfortable experimenting with |

Once funded, you're ready for [your first deposit](/docs/getting-started/your-first-deposit).
