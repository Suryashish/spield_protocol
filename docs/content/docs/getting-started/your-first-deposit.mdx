You're connected and funded — time to deposit. The **simplest** first experience is the
**Fixed-Rate Vault**: deposit USDC, get a receipt for a guaranteed payout at maturity. You
never touch PT or YT directly.

## The easy path: the Fixed-Rate Vault

<Steps>

<Step>
### Open the Fixed Vault

In the Spield app sidebar, choose **Fixed Vault**.
</Step>

<Step>
### Enter an amount

Type how much USDC you want to deposit. The app shows a **live quote**: your guaranteed
**payout at maturity** (principal + coupon) and the **fixed APY** you're locking in.
</Step>

<Step>
### Review and deposit

Check the quote, then click **Deposit**. Approve the transaction(s) in your wallet — the
first deposit may include a one-time trustline approval.
</Step>

<Step>
### Hold your receipt

You receive a **receipt** representing your guaranteed payout. Hold it until maturity, then
redeem it for the full payout in USDC.
</Step>

</Steps>

<Callout title="That's it — your return is locked">
  From this moment your payout is fixed. Market rates can move all they like; your receipt
  pays out the same amount at maturity regardless.
</Callout>

## Prefer to hold the tokens directly?

If you want PT and YT in your own wallet (to trade, to provide liquidity, or to claim yield
yourself), use the **Deposit** page instead. It mints equal amounts of **PT** (your fixed
bond) and **YT** (the yield claim) straight to your wallet.

That's the more advanced path — see [How It Works](/docs/how-it-works) and the
[guides](/docs/guides) to decide what to do with each token.

## What happens after you deposit?

| Product | What you hold | What you do later |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Fixed-Rate Vault** | A receipt for a fixed payout | Redeem the receipt at maturity for USDC. |
| **Deposit (PT + YT)** | PT and YT in your wallet | Hold PT to maturity, claim YT yield over time, or trade either on the market. |

## Tracking your position

The app's **Overview** shows your balances and performance, and the **Solvency** page proves
in real time that the protocol's backing covers everything it owes. You can watch your
position grow there at any time.
