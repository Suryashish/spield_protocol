Before your Stellar wallet can hold a particular asset, it needs a **trustline** to that asset.
This is a normal, one-time part of using any Stellar token — and for Spield's tokens, the app
handles it for you with a single click. Here's exactly what it means and how it works.

## What is a trustline?

On Stellar, an account doesn't automatically hold every asset in existence. Instead, it
explicitly opts in to the assets it's willing to hold by creating a **trustline** to each one.
Think of it as ticking a box that says *"yes, I'm willing to receive and hold this token."*

- Without a trustline to an asset, your wallet **cannot receive** that asset — a transfer would
  simply fail.
- With a trustline, the asset shows up in your wallet and you can hold, send, and use it
  normally.

<Callout title="Why Stellar works this way">
  Requiring an explicit opt-in keeps wallets tidy and protects you from being spammed with
  unwanted tokens. It's a deliberate Stellar design choice, not a Spield-specific step.
</Callout>

## Which Spield assets need a trustline?

Spield's two tokens are real Stellar assets, so your account needs a trustline to each before it
can receive them:

| Asset | Code | When you need it |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Principal Token** | `SPLDPT` | Before you can be minted or receive PT. |
| **Yield Token** | `SPLDYT` | Before you can be minted or receive YT. |

USDC also uses a trustline, but you'll typically already have it from when you first received
USDC (from an exchange or [the bridge](/docs/getting-started/funding-your-wallet)).

## You don't set these up manually — the app does

You **don't** need to add the PT/YT trustlines by hand. The first time you do something that
requires them — your first **deposit** or **mint** — the Spield app shows an **"Enable
trustline"** button. Clicking it sets up the `SPLDPT` and `SPLDYT` trustlines for your wallet in
one approval.

<Steps>

<Step>
### Start your first deposit

On the [Deposit](/docs/features/pt-yt-tokens) or [Fixed Vault](/docs/features/fixed-rate-vault)
page, enter an amount. If your wallet doesn't yet have the PT/YT trustlines, the app detects
this automatically.
</Step>

<Step>
### Click "Enable trustline"

The app shows an **Enable trustline** button. Click it — this is the one-time opt-in for the
`SPLDPT` and `SPLDYT` assets.
</Step>

<Step>
### Approve in your wallet

Your wallet pops up to approve adding the trustlines. Approve it. This is a standard Stellar
operation, not a transfer of funds.
</Step>

<Step>
### Continue depositing

With trustlines in place, your deposit proceeds and the PT/YT tokens land in your wallet. You
**won't be asked again** — trustlines are a one-time setup per asset.
</Step>

</Steps>

<Callout type="info" title="It only happens once">
  After the first time, your wallet keeps the PT/YT trustlines, so every future deposit skips
  this step entirely. If you ever connect a brand-new wallet, you'll do the one-click setup once
  more for that wallet.
</Callout>

## What does a trustline cost?

A trustline reserves a tiny amount of **XLM** (Stellar's native asset) — a small, **refundable**
amount that's locked while the trustline exists and returned if you ever remove it. This is why
you keep a little XLM in your wallet (see [funding your wallet](/docs/getting-started/funding-your-wallet)).
It is not a fee paid to Spield; it's a standard Stellar account reserve.

## Troubleshooting

| Symptom | Likely cause | Fix |
| --- | --- | --- |
| "Enable trustline" keeps appearing | The trustline transaction wasn't approved/confirmed | Re-click and approve in your wallet; ensure you have a little XLM for the reserve. |
| Deposit fails before you can deposit | Missing PT/YT trustline | Use the **Enable trustline** button first. |
| Not enough XLM | No XLM for the reserve/fee | Add a small amount of XLM (see [funding](/docs/getting-started/funding-your-wallet)). |

Once your trustlines are set, you're ready for [your first deposit](/docs/getting-started/your-first-deposit).
