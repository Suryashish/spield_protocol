To use Spield you connect a **Stellar wallet** — a browser extension (or web wallet) that
holds your keys and signs transactions. You stay in control: Spield never has access to your
secret key, and every action requires your approval in the wallet.

## Supported wallets

Spield works with the major Stellar wallets:

| Wallet | Type | Notes |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Freighter** | Browser extension | The most widely used Stellar wallet; a great default. |
| **xBull** | Extension / web | Feature-rich, supports hardware wallets. |
| **Rabet** | Browser extension | Lightweight and simple. |
| **Hana** | Browser extension | Multi-chain wallet with Stellar support. |
| **LOBSTR** | Extension (signer) | Popular mobile-first wallet; uses its signer extension on desktop. |
| **Albedo** | Web-based | Nothing to install — signs in a popup window. |

<Callout type="info">
  Don't have one yet? **Freighter** is the simplest place to start. Install it, create a
  wallet, and back up your recovery phrase somewhere safe.
</Callout>

## Connect to Spield

<Steps>

<Step>
### Install a wallet

Add a wallet extension from its official source and create (or import) an account. Write
down your recovery phrase and keep it offline.
</Step>

<Step>
### Open the Spield app

Go to the Spield app and click **Connect Wallet** in the top-right corner.
</Step>

<Step>
### Choose your wallet

Pick your wallet from the list. Installed wallets are highlighted; web wallets like Albedo
always work.
</Step>

<Step>
### Approve the connection

Your wallet pops up asking to connect. Approve it. Spield now shows your address and
balances — you're connected.
</Step>

</Steps>

## Make sure you're on the right network

<DevPhaseNote />

Spield is currently **live on Stellar testnet**, with **mainnet launching soon**. Set your
wallet to the network the app is on (today: **Testnet**), so you can try everything with free
test funds. Most wallets switch networks from their settings in one click, and Spield will
prompt you if your wallet is on the wrong one.

When mainnet goes live, the same flow applies on the **Stellar public network** — just switch
your wallet to it.

<Callout type="warn" title="Always verify the URL">
  Before connecting, double-check you're on the official Spield app domain. Bookmark it.
  Wallet phishing usually relies on look-alike URLs — never enter your recovery phrase into
  any website, ever. A legitimate site will only ask you to *approve transactions* in your
  wallet's own popup.
</Callout>

## Disconnecting

Click your address in the top-right and choose **Disconnect**. This just forgets the session
in your browser — your funds and wallet are unaffected.
