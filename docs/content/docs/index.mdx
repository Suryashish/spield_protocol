**Spield** turns real, on-chain Stellar yield into building blocks anyone can use:
a **fixed-rate bond** you can hold to a guaranteed return, a **leveraged yield token**
for those who want upside, and a **trading venue** where both change hands.

If you have ten seconds: deposit USDC, choose **certainty**, **leverage**, or **fees** — and
the protocol does the rest, with backing that provably grows on-chain.

## New here? Start with the idea

Most yield in DeFi is **variable** — it moves with the market, and you never know
exactly what you'll earn. Spield lets you **fix it**: lock a known return by a known
date, the same way a bond works in traditional finance. Read [What is Spield?](/docs/introduction)
for the full picture, or jump straight to [Earn a fixed yield](/docs/guides/earn-fixed-yield).

## Want to build on it?

Spield's tokens (PT and YT) and its contracts are open and composable. The
[Developers](/docs/developers) section covers the architecture, how to read live
protocol state, and how to integrate Spield into your own app — without needing to
understand the internals.
